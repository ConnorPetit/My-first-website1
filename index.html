About Me

Hi my name is Connor Petit, welcome to my portfolio. I am 20 years old and attend San Jacinto College. My work includes digital artwork, photography, videography, and branding design. I specialize in Adobe products including Illustrator, Photoshop, Lightroom, and Premiere Pro. This portfolio focuses on my portfolio for my photography.

I currently live in League City, Texas and will to eventually move into Houston in the future. Design and photography have always been a hobby to me, so I decided to make it a carrer. I enjoy taking pictures, and the feeling of pride when the outcome looks amazing. I will expand more portfolio as I gain more expierence taking pictures of my everyday life, clients, and friends.

My goal for my carrer is to be a succesful photographer and designer. I aim to make my clients happy and make work that is visually appealing to the viewer. One big goal of mine is to fly to diiferent countrys to take picutres of different cultures around the world, especially Japan and Italy. These goals I expect to complete in the next 10 years, I will create a plan to find success in my carrer path.
